Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 May 2026

focuscube ("we", "the service") is a minimal focus timer running at focuscube.app. This policy explains the small amount of personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

1. What we collect

focuscube only collects what is needed to run the service:

  • Account data: when you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your email address, display name, and profile picture URL from the provider. We never receive your password.
  • Focus session data: when you complete a timer session, we store the mode (e.g. 25), the duration in seconds, and a timestamp. This is used to compute your stats and streak.
  • Session cookies: an HTTP-only cookie containing a session id, used to keep you signed in. It is deleted when the session expires or when you sign out.
  • Anonymous analytics (via Google Analytics 4): page views, country, device class, and referrer. No personally identifying data is sent. You can opt out by enabling "Do Not Track" or by blocking the analytics script.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect IP addresses for tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not contact your contacts or read your email.

3. Where data is stored

Account data and focus session data are stored in Cloudflare D1 (an edge-replicated SQLite database) under our Cloudflare account. The service runs on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare Pages.

4. Cookies

We use one essential cookie (focuscube_session) to keep you signed in. It is HTTP-only, secure, and scoped to .focuscube.app. Analytics cookies are managed by Google Analytics; see Google's policy.

5. Your rights

You can:

  • Sign out at any time, which deletes your session cookie.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing abdulahcicekli@gmail.com.
  • Request full deletion of your account and all focus session data by emailing the same address. We honor deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Children

focuscube is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions: abdulahcicekli@gmail.com.
